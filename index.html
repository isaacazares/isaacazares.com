About

Isaac Cázares

Isaac is a Filmmaker and Visual Storyteller from San Luis Potosí, Mexico, he graduated from Communications and Digital Media at Tecnológico de Monterrey in Mexico City.

Over the past years he has been working on international projects, telling compelling stories and producing high quality content, featuring brands such as Vice, Vice News, Netflix, National Geographic, NFL, among others.

Isaac is available for worldwide projects in order to fit your brand’s specific needs. Send him an email to hola@isaacazares.com and he’ll get in touch with you as soon as possible!
